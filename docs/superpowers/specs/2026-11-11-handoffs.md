# Addressed handoffs

**Problem.** Harnesses come to M.O.T.H.E.R, but nothing lets work started in one harness wait for a specific other one.

**Decision.** `mother_assign_task` names a receiving harness (its grant name) for an open task. That harness sees the task under `assignedToYou` in context and at the top of its brief, does the work under its own permissions and reports through normal task statuses. Context lists active harness names for non-shared clients.

**Unchanged.** Record shapes, task statuses, scopes, and the rule that M.O.T.H.E.R executes nothing. Assignments live in an additive table that older executables ignore.

**Not included.** Waking or launching a harness, notifications, a model of her own that chooses harnesses, descriptions of what each harness is good at, and handoffs of kinds other than tasks.

**Acceptance.** `tests/handoffs.rs` and the handoff stdio test in `tests/mcp.rs`.
