Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to move closer, and click a flower to visit it.

The records are the large blooms standing above the wild flowers, each catching a glint of sun. They grow in six loose drifts, one per kind, and each kind is its own flower: lilac daisies are memories, mint tulips decisions, orange poppies tasks, blue bellflowers checkpoints, golden sunflowers artifacts and pink roses actions. Taller stems have been revised more often.

The small wild flowers, grass and butterflies are only the field. Near each drift the wild flowers lean towards its colour.

The lotuses on the pond around the candle are the pinned core, which every harness reads first.

A bloom that slowly swells, with a ring opening around it, is a task that is in progress or blocked, or an action whose outcome is pending or unknown. Faded nodding buds are retracted, superseded or cancelled.

A solid white thread is a saved link. A dotted one means two records are similar in meaning.

The slider replays the week. Everything here is invented example data, and the field is read-only.
